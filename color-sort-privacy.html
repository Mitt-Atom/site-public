Privacy Policy — Color Sort Puzzle

Effective date: 2026-05-18

This privacy policy applies to the Color Sort Puzzle app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by LLC "MITT ATOM" (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a free-to-play service that contains advertisements and in-app purchases. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

How the Application Handles Data

The Application does not operate any backend servers owned or controlled by the Service Provider. The Service Provider does not collect, receive, or store any personal data on its own infrastructure. All game progress and settings are stored locally on your device.

However, the Application embeds third-party SDKs provided by Google (Google AdMob for advertising and Google Play Billing for in-app purchases). These SDKs collect data directly from your device for their own purposes, as described below. The Service Provider does not have access to that data beyond the aggregated reports Google provides.

The Application does not gather precise location information about your mobile device.

The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or provide features.

In-App Purchases and Payments

The Application offers in-app purchases (such as removing advertisements). All financial transactions and payments are processed securely and directly through the Google Play Billing system. The Service Provider does not collect, store, or have access to your financial information, credit card numbers, or billing details. Purchase history and entitlement validation are managed by Google within your Google account.

Advertising

The Application displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, AdMob may collect data from your device, including advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, coarse device and network information, and information about your interaction with ads and the Application. This data is collected and processed by Google under its own privacy policy.

On Android 13 and above, you can control personalised advertising through your device settings. You can also opt out of personalised ads at any time via your Google account settings.

Third-Party Services

The Application uses the following third-party services. Each operates under its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to review:

  • Google Privacy Policy (covers Google Play Services and Google Play Billing)
  • Google AdMob Privacy & Terms

  • when the Service Provider believes in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect their rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request.

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the Application easily by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall processes as may be available as part of your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace or network.

Data Retention Policy

Since the Application operates without a cloud backend, all game progress and purchase validation histories are stored locally on your device or managed securely within your Google Play account. The Service Provider does not store your personal data on external servers. If you wish to delete your local data, you can do so by clearing the Application's cache or uninstalling the app from your device.

Children's Privacy

The Application is designed for a mixed audience, which may include children under the age of 13. We are committed to protecting the privacy of all users, and we follow the requirements of COPPA, GDPR-K, and the Google Play Families Policy.

On first launch, the Application presents a Neutral Age Screen. If a user identifies themselves as under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in their region):

  • We do not collect or request any personally identifiable information.
  • We request only non-personalised, child-directed advertising from Google AdMob by signalling child-directed treatment and use only Google-certified ad partners for that traffic.
  • We do not enable behavioural tracking or interest-based advertising for that user.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has somehow provided us with personal information without your consent, please contact us at info@mittatom.com, and we will take immediate steps to remove such data.

Security

Because the Application does not transmit data to servers operated by the Service Provider, there is no user data held by the Service Provider that could be exposed by a breach on our side. All Application data is stored locally on your device, and its security depends on the security of your device and your Google account. Data handled by Google AdMob and Google Play Billing is protected by Google's own security measures, as described in their respective policies.
